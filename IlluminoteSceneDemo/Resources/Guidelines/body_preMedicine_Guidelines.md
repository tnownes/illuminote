#### Medical: Body Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Evaluate the substantive core of the applicant's narrative. Determine if they effectively demonstrate core medical competencies through deep self-reflection on specific experiences.

* **Reflection Over Recitation:** Strictly flag paragraphs that read like a "narrative resume" or a chronological list of CV activities. Advise the user to focus on what they learned, how they felt, and how they grew from one or two specific experiences.

* **"Show, Don't Tell" Competencies:** Scan for concrete, behavioral examples demonstrating competencies: altruism, compassion, empathy, integrity, cultural competency, and ethical grounding. Praise the applicant when they illustrate these traits through actions.

* **Emotional Boundaries:** Point out if the tone becomes inappropriately "confessional," overly sentimental, or melodramatic. The essay should be written for a smart layperson, so flag overly technical jargon or dense research descriptions.

* **Integration Check (Narrative Flow):** Check the first sentence for a smooth transition. Ask the AI to evaluate if this paragraph acts as a logical building block in the overall narrative. Does it connect to the theme introduced in the opening, or does it feel like a disjointed, copy-pasted journal entry?
