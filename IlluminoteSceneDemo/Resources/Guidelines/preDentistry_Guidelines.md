### Dental School Personal Statement Guidelines

**Overall Assessment**

* **The "Why Dentistry" Narrative:** Evaluate whether the motivation is clear, durable, and supported by real experiences.
* **General Applicability:** Ensure claims are broadly applicable and not tied to one specific school.
* **Style and Tone:** Assess for positive, mature, and forward-looking voice that reflects resilience and professionalism.
* **Red-Flag Tone:** Flag whining, defensiveness, blame-shifting, or polarizing commentary unrelated to dentistry.

**Opening Paragraph**

* **Engaging Hook:** Reward openings grounded in a concrete patient, shadowing, or formative moment.
* **Cliché Detection:** Critique generic openings that rely on broad "science + helping people" statements without depth.
* **Narrative Setup:** Confirm the introduction sets a coherent trajectory for the body paragraphs.

**Body Paragraphs**

* **Reflection Over Recitation:** Flag resume-like sequencing of activities with minimal interpretation.
* **Preparedness Signals:** Look for evidence of chairside exposure, patient interaction, service, and sustained career exploration.
* **Personal Qualities:** Scan for credible examples of maturity, empathy, dexterity, communication, and teamwork.
* **Unique Angle:** Encourage meaningful integration of background factors (e.g., culture, language, artistry) only when tied to patient care and fit.

**Closing Paragraph**

* **Narrative Synthesis:** Evaluate whether the ending integrates key experiences into a clear dental purpose.
* **Professional Tone Check:** Flag arrogance or grandiose claims and push toward grounded confidence.
* **Future Orientation:** Confirm the close looks ahead with realistic readiness, not abstract aspiration.
