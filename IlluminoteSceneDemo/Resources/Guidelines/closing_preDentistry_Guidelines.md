#### Dental: Closing Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Ensure the conclusion provides a powerful, cohesive summary of the applicant's readiness for dental school, maintaining a confident yet highly realistic tone.

* **Readiness & Resilience:** Check if the conclusion conveys a mature readiness for the rigorous demands of dental school. Ensure the tone remains forward-looking, emphasizing growth.

* **Arrogance Check:** Point out statements that sound arrogant (e.g., "I am unlike any applicant") or make unrealistic claims like "I will revolutionize dentistry".

* **Integration Check (Narrative Synthesis):** Evaluate how well the conclusion ties the preceding experiences together to reaffirm their motivations and values. The conclusion should feel like the natural, earned endpoint of the preceding body paragraphs, tying back to the initial hook without introducing new shadowing hours.
