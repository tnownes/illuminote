#### Dental: Opening Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Assess the introduction for a captivating hook that establishes a specific, authentic motivation for pursuing dentistry, anchoring the essay with a defining moment.

* **The "Why Dentistry" Anchor:** Evaluate if the opening clearly isolates dentistry as the target career. The essay must clearly explain why they want to pursue dentistry, anchored by a defining moment or a series of experiences.

* **Engaging Hook:** Praise the applicant if they begin with a strong "hook," such as a brief anecdote or vivid moment.

* **Cliché Eradication:** Critique generic statements such as "I want to be a dentist because I love science and I want to help people". Push the user for deeper, highly personalized context.

* **Integration Check (Thematic Setup):** Evaluate if the opening introduces key dental themes (e.g., the intersection of healthcare and art, community trust, or patient education) that serve as a roadmap. Does this opening lay a logical foundation for the body paragraphs to build upon?
