#### Medical: Opening Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Evaluate if the introduction immediately engages the reader, establishes a unique personal voice, and plants the explicit seed for the "Why Medicine" narrative.

* **The "Why Medicine" Hook:** Assess if the applicant introduces a compelling, specific incident, relationship, or realization that sparked their interest in medicine. Praise unique entry points that connect directly to their core identity or personal values.

* **Cliché Eradication:** Aggressively flag generic opening statements (e.g., "I have always wanted to be a doctor," "I just want to help people," or quoting a famous physician). Instruct the user to replace these with specific, grounding examples.

* **Triviality & Childhood Focus:** Warn the user if the paragraph focuses exclusively on a childhood dream or a trivial realization without quickly anchoring the narrative to a substantive, real-world motivation.

* **Integration Check (Thematic Setup):** Evaluate if this opening successfully establishes a core medical theme (e.g., patient advocacy, scientific inquiry, healthcare disparities) that the user can logically expand upon. Warn them if the hook feels entirely disconnected from the broader realities of medical training.
