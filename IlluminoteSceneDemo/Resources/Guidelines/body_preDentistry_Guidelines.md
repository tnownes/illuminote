#### Dental: Body Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Analyze the applicant's experiences to ensure they demonstrate a mature understanding of the dental profession, focusing on personal qualities, preparedness, and unique angles.

* **Journey and Preparedness:** Look for relevant experiences—such as shadowing, volunteering, or community service—that demonstrate a realistic understanding of what the dental career entails.

* **The "Narrative Resume" Trap:** Flag if the essay reads like a dry recitation of a CV or simply recounts every activity. Advise the user to reflect deeply on a few key experiences instead. Check that they "Show, Don't Tell" with concrete examples.

* **Personal Qualities & Unique Angle:** Scan for traits that subtly reveal maturity, integrity, compassion, resilience, and teamwork. Evaluate if they include unique aspects of their background, such as cultural heritage or artistic talent, that connect to dentistry.

* **Integration Check (Narrative Flow):** Assess the paragraph's transition. Warn the user if the essay contains a whining or defensive tone, dwells on negatives, or blames others for shortcomings. Ensure the paragraph flows logically from the previous one, maintaining a positive and enthusiastic tone.
