#### Medical: Closing Paragraph Guidelines
* **Primary Objective:** Evaluate how effectively the conclusion synthesizes the applicant's journey, reaffirms their commitment to medicine, and projects their future impact without arrogance.

* **Future Vision & Humility:** Evaluate if the applicant articulates a realistic, mature vision for their future. The tone should strike a balance between being confident and humble, strictly avoiding arrogance.

* **Grandiose Claims & Savior Complex:** Strictly flag statements that exhibit a melodramatic savior complex (e.g., "medicine is my one true calling to save the world").

* **Integration Check (Narrative Synthesis):** Check if the conclusion seamlessly ties together the themes, values, and experiences discussed in the preceding paragraphs to reaffirm exactly what the applicant will bring to the medical field. Warn the user if they introduce entirely new clinical experiences or extracurriculars in the final sentences.
