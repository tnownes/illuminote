# Illuminote Workspace Notes

## Project Overview

Illuminote is an iPhone-first SwiftUI app for guided reflection, journaling, and personal-statement support. The primary v1 audience is pre-health students, especially Catholic or spiritually engaged students, using reflection to make meaning of experiences and later turn that reflection into application-ready writing.

The app blends Examen-inspired reflection, journaling, and draft-building. It should feel emotionally serious, calm, and trustworthy rather than productivity-driven, generic-AI, or consumer-wellness styled.

## Implementation Preferences

- Prefer existing design-system tokens and shared components before introducing new styling primitives.
- Keep `Sacred Void` as the signature atmosphere for Examen, reflection, and AI-assisted writing moments, but use clearer and more functional surfaces for organizational screens.
- Protect readability and accessibility at all times: high contrast, stable reading surfaces, generous spacing, obvious touch targets, strong VoiceOver order, and reduced-motion support.
- Avoid generic AI writing-tool aesthetics, productivity-dashboard layouts, stock serenity tropes, church-bulletin styling, and admissions-portal visual language.

## Design Context

### Users
Illuminote is optimized for iPhone-first pre-health students, especially Catholic or spiritually engaged students, who use reflection to make meaning of their experiences and later turn that reflection into application-ready writing.

This user is thoughtful but busy, often emotionally overloaded, serious about vocation, service, and calling, and needs an interface that lowers cognitive load without flattening the emotional depth of the work.

### Brand Personality
Contemplative, trustworthy, luminous.

The interface should evoke calm, clarity, reverence, grounded hope, and quiet confidence. The tone should feel welcoming, human, and discerning. It should remain clearly Ignatian-rooted without feeling exclusionary, overly ecclesial, or spiritually performative.

### Aesthetic Direction
`Sacred Void` remains the emotional core identity of the app, especially for Examen, reflection, and AI-assisted writing moments. The broader app should be cleaner and more functional around that core, using calmer support surfaces where clarity matters more than atmosphere.

The design should borrow Apple Journal's restraint, spaciousness, and emotional dignity, along with the calm structural clarity of Apple Mindfulness and Health. It can borrow a little of Headspace's pacing philosophy, but not its playful or consumer-wellness visual language.

The app should not look like a generic AI writing tool, a productivity dashboard, a stock-serenity wellness app, a church bulletin or parish CMS, or a premed admissions portal.

Use strong text contrast, one restrained gold or light accent, dignified and readable typography, generous spacing, obvious touch targets, excellent VoiceOver order and labels, and slow intentional opacity-led transitions. Avoid unreadable animated backgrounds behind text, decorative display fonts for primary reading, low-contrast glass-on-glass surfaces, motion that slides or performs, too many simultaneous accent colors, and repetitive overexplaining of the spiritual frame.

### Design Principles
- Design for contemplative focus first. The interface should help users slow down, notice, and reflect rather than rush, optimize, or perform.
- Keep Ignatian structure while using welcoming language. The app can be prayerful and discernment-oriented without feeling exclusionary or overly institutional.
- Use `Sacred Void` as the soul of the product, not the default treatment for every screen. Reflection moments can feel atmospheric; organizational screens should stay clearer and more functional.
- Support serious writing and reflection with dignity. Favor readable typography, strong hierarchy, clean spacing, and obvious interaction patterns over decorative flourish.
- Treat accessibility as part of the aesthetic. High contrast, stable reading surfaces, reduced visual noise, generous touch targets, and strong VoiceOver behavior should reinforce trust and calm.
